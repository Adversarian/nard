GNU Backgammon 1.08.003
========================

nard bundles the official GNU Backgammon 1.08.003 Windows program and invokes
it as a separate process over a line-delimited JSON/text protocol.

GNU Backgammon is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3.
The complete GPL-3.0 text is installed beside the program as gnubg/COPYING.

Official Windows binary:
https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/gnubg/gnubg-1_08_003-20240428-setup.exe

Corresponding source:
https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/gnubg/gnubg-release-1.08.003-sources.tar.gz

Project home:
https://www.gnu.org/software/gnubg/

No GNU Backgammon source is linked into or copied into nard's packages.
