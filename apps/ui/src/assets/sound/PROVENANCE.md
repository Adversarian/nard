required and none is owed — the credits below are recorded because knowing where
an asset came from is worth more than the licence obliges.

**Do not add audio under any other licence.** This repository is MIT and public.
CC-BY would drag an attribution obligation into a binary we ship; CC-BY-NC is
outright incompatible. If a sound is not CC0, it does not go in.

## Why these

The board is bone and wood, so **checker placement is a wood impact**, not a
plastic click — that is the sound heard most often and the one that has to be
right. Hits and bear-offs are disc-on-disc rather than disc-on-board, so the
casino chip samples are the correct physics even though the material is not: a
checker landing on a stack of checkers does not sound like wood on a point.

Every repeated event has four or more variants. A single sample played
identically sixty times a game reads as a rattle, which is worse than silence.

## Not from a recording

An earlier draft of `docs/sound-spec.md` called for recording real equipment.
That was dropped in favour of CC0 libraries. If it ever changes, the wood
impacts are the ones worth re-recording; the chip sounds are already right.
